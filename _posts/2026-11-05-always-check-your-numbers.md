---
title: "Always Check Your Numbers"
author: Xavier
---

Something I learned about coding: if you set the speed too high, it goes way
too fast. So make sure to check your numbers. One time, the robot's speed was
set to 10 instead of 1, and it crashed into the mission and knocked it over!
But we all laughed. After that, we set the mission back up, changed the speed
to 1, and fixed it. Now we know to always check our numbers.
