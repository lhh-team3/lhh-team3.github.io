We're the Atomic Ants, a FIRST LEGO League Challenge team at Lansing Tech
Studio. This season we designed Buggy 12, an underwater drone that slurps up
invasive zebra mussels from the lake bottom, grinds them up, and sends the
pieces to the surface.

Watch our video on the [project design](/design/) page, or see our
[blueprints](/2026/10/14/buggy-12-blueprints/).
