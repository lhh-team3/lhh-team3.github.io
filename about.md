## This season

Our Innovation Project is Buggy 12, an underwater drone that slurps up invasive
zebra mussels from the lake bottom, grinds them up, and sends the pieces to the
surface. Zebra mussels clog pipes, boats, and water intakes. We drew a first
blueprint, then updated it to version 2.0 with feedback from our experts.

Watch our video on the [project design](/design/) page, see our
[blueprints](/2026/10/14/buggy-12-blueprints/), or read what we learned about
[checking our numbers](/2026/11/05/always-check-your-numbers/).
